Dermal Fillers

Lift. Define. Restore.

At SKN Lab Aesthetics®, dermal fillers are used to restore balance, enhance natural contours and soften the visible signs of ageing - without changing who you are.

As we age, collagen, elastin and facial volume gradually diminish, leaving the face less defined and the skin less supported. Expertly placed dermal fillers can lift, smooth and subtly recontour, bringing structure and vitality back to your features.

What can fillers do?

Restore lost volume in the cheeks and temples

Define the chin and jawline

Refresh tired eyes with tear trough correction

Refine and balance the nose without surgery

Rebuild mid-face structure for a natural lift

Add soft, hydrated definition to the lips

Formulated with Hyaluronic Acid, a naturally occurring molecule in the skin, fillers attract and retain moisture — improving hydration and suppleness while restoring youthful structure.

At SKN Lab Aesthetics®, filler isn’t about trends or “mls” - it’s about artistry. We design every treatment around your facial proportions, skin quality and goals for results that are elegant, balanced and authentically you.

TREATMENT AREAS

Cheeks

Lift and contour the mid-face to restore structure and radiance. Enhances youthful curves and supports surrounding features.

Tear Troughs

Brighten tired eyes and restore smoothness to the under-eye area with advanced, delicate techniques .

Jawline

Sharpen or soften definition, reduce jowls and create clean lower-face contours.

Lips

Shape, symmetry and hydration tailored to your anatomy. We don’t measure lips by millilitres — every syringe is bespoke, used only for you, ensuring precision, safety and natural harmony.

Chin

Refine or project the chin to balance facial proportions. Perfect for creating definition and smoothing dimpling.

Nose (Non-Surgical Rhinoplasty)

Balance and refine nasal shape using precision filler placement — lift a tip, smooth a bump, or subtly contour for elegant, natural correction.

Temples

Replace lost upper-face volume to lift and balance the entire face — a subtle enhancement that creates a refreshed, rested appearance.

Full-Face Contour & Lift

Comprehensive rejuvenation that restores structure and balance across multiple areas, enhancing your natural bone structure and definition for seamless, sculpted results.
