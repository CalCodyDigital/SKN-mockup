SKN Membership T&C

These terms and conditions apply to all SKN Lab Aesthetics memberships, including the Botox Membership (SKN Smooth Membership) and the SKN Rejuvenation Membership. By enrolling in our membership programs, you agree to the following:

1) Membership Payment

The membership fee is payable monthly by direct debit or other automated payment methods.

Clients have the option to pay for the full membership upfront, which includes a discounted price of £900 for the SKN Smooth Membership and £864 for the SKN Rejuvenation Membership.

Missed monthly payments will result in the suspension of membership benefits until the outstanding amount is cleared.

If a payment is missed for more than 14 days, SKN Lab reserves the right to terminate the membership, and any unused treatments will be forfeited.

Failure to attend a scheduled appointment without prior cancellation or rescheduling within the 48-hour notice period will result in the forfeit of that appointment, and no rescheduling or refund will be offered. Full payment is still applicable, as each appointment within the membership holds a monetary value.

Gift Voucher Scheme: Memberships can be purchased as part of the SKN Lab Gift Voucher scheme but must be paid upfront in full. The membership is valid for only one person and cannot be shared.

Outstanding Payments: Any outstanding amounts owed to SKN Lab that remain unpaid will be pursued through debt collectors or in a small claims court to recover the debt​

2) Appointment Scheduling

All appointments under the membership must be scheduled in advance upon commencement of the membership.

Botox Membership: Appointments are scheduled every 12 weeks and must adhere strictly to this timeline.

SKN Rejuvenation Membership: Profhilo sessions will be booked 4 weeks apart, and microneedling sessions will be booked 4-6 weeks apart.

Rescheduled appointments must remain within the prescribed 12-week timeframe (for Botox) or 4-6 week timeframe (for the Rejuvenation Membership). No early appointments can be administered before the 12-week or 4-6 week periods from the start of treatment.

3) Cancellation policy

Any appointment cancelled within 48 hours of the scheduled time will result in the forfeiture of that appointment. No refunds, rebooking, or discounts will be offered for missed appointments.

In cases of emergency, please contact SKN Lab as soon as possible, and while the cancellation policy remains, exceptions may be considered at SKN Lab’s discretion.

4. Review Appointments

A review appointment for all Botox treatments must be booked 14 days post-initial treatment to assess results and ensure optimal outcomes. Failure to attend the review appointment within the designated timeframe may impact the treatment results.

Review appointments must also be scheduled upon membership commencement, and it is the client’s responsibility to attend at the designated time.

Review appointments are the only time where additional product may be administered. There is a possibility that you may be have movement after your review appointment. These appointments may vary in result and may not entirely stop all movement but to help lines from forming and reduce the appearance of static lines.

5. Consultations

Clients are required to undertake a consultation twice during the membership period to ensure treatment eligibility and ongoing suitability. Failure to attend these consultations may result in the termination of the membership without refund.

6. Membership Duration & Validity

Memberships are valid for 12 months from the start date, covering four Botox appointments (Botox Membership) or two Profhilo and three microneedling appointments (SKN Rejuvenation Membership).

Early termination of the membership, either by the client or SKN Lab due to breach of terms, will not result in a refund of any previously paid membership fees, and all unused treatments will be forfeited.

7 Rescheduling Appointments

Any rescheduled appointments must fall within the designated treatment intervals:

Botox appointments: strictly every 12 weeks.

Profhilo and microneedling sessions: as per the treatment plan.

Early or late appointments outside these intervals will not be permitted, and the client risks forfeiting their next scheduled appointment if rescheduling does not comply with these timelines.

8 Refund Policy

Membership fees are non-refundable, and no refund or discount will be given for missed appointments or cancellations made outside the appropriate timeframe.

SKN Lab does not offer partial refunds for unused treatments if the membership is terminated early.

£828 for the SKN Smooth Membership (Botox)

£864 for the SKN Rejuvenation Membership
