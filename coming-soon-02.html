Looking for Endolift?

At SKN Lab Aesthetics, we use Endoform by Neoform Technologies, one of the newest laser devices available in the UK.

When your reflection no longer

matches how you feel...

There often isn't a dramatic moment.

One day you catch your reflection in a shop window, see a photo of yourself from the side, or glance in the mirror and realise something has changed.

Your jawline isn't as defined.

Your neck looks softer.

Your face feels heavier than it used to.

You've tried injectables.

You've lost weight.

You've exercised.

You may have even tried facial massage, gua sha, taping or every jawline hack social media could throw at you.

Yet that stubborn fullness beneath the chin remains.

The jowls continue to form.

The jawline that once felt sharp and defined becomes harder to see.

That's because ageing isn't just about the skin.

Over time, collagen declines, support structures weaken and small pockets of fat can accumulate or descend, changing the way the face is supported.

Endoform is designed to address these structural changes from within, helping to tighten tissue, remodel localised fat and stimulate new collagen production without surgery.

Why Endoform Is Different?

Most skin tightening treatments work from the outside in, delivering energy through the skin's surface and into the tissues beneath.

Endoform takes a different approach.

Using an ultra-fine optical fibre placed beneath the skin, laser energy is delivered directly into the structures responsible for facial support, contour and skin firmness.

This allows Endoform to address multiple causes of facial ageing in a single treatment.

✔ Tighten weakened support structures

✔ Stimulate new collagen production

✔ Improve skin firmness and elasticity

✔ Remodel localised fat

✔ Restore facial definition and contour

Rather than simply treating the surface of the skin, Endoform addresses the structural changes that contribute to jowls, lower face heaviness and loss of definition over time.
