Because great skin starts with understanding yours

At SKN Lab Aesthetics® in Ware, every journey begins with a consultation. It’s where we take the time to understand your skin, your lifestyle, and your goals, so that we can create results that feel effortless, natural, and completely you.

Whether you’re new to aesthetics or looking to refine your results, our consultations are designed to bring clarity and confidence. No confusion, no guesswork — just expert guidance and a bespoke plan created around you.

Our Consultations are Different...

With over eight decades of combined experience across medical nursing and advanced aesthetics , our team brings unrivalled expertise to every client. We’re known for our calm, professional approach that feels welcoming rather than clinical, blending medical knowledge with the artistry of advanced aesthetic techniques.

We don’t do one-size-fits-all. Every face tells a different story , and our goal is to enhance yours with precision and care. During your consultation, we’ll assess your skin , listen to your concerns, and create a tailored plan that helps you look and feel your absolute best.

We understand that aesthetics is a commitment — in time, trust , and investment. During your consultation , we’ll discuss a plan that fits both your goals and your comfort, so you always feel in control of your journey.

You’ll leave feeling informed , supported, and excited to begin your SKN journey.

Your Consultation Options

General Treatment Consultation

Perfect for anyone unsure where to start. If you’re considering aesthetic or skin treatments but aren’t sure what’s right for you , this consultation will help guide you. We’ll discuss your skin health, lifestyle, and long-term goals before recommending the most effective options to rejuvenate and refresh your appearance naturally.

Prescription Consultation

For prescriptive treatments such as anti-wrinkle injections. Our prescriber-led practitioners will assess your suitability for treatment, discuss your medical history , and create a precise, safe plan designed around your goals. Every appointment prioritises subtle, natural results that enhance, not change, your features.

Laser Consultation

For IPL Hair Removal, tattoo removal, and carbon laser treatments . This consultation ensures your skin is suitable for laser and includes a patch test . We’ll analyse your skin type, discuss desired outcomes , and design a bespoke laser treatment plan to deliver visible, lasting results with minimal downtime.
