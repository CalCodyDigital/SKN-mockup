Microneedling & Meso Therapy

Micro Needling is a great way to rejuvenate and tighten the skin, Reduce Scarring including stretch marks and acne scarring, reduce fine lines and wrinkles, reduce pigmentation and tighten loose, sagging skin.

At SKN Lab Aesthetics we favour power assisted micro needling because of the controlled and speedy accuracy it delivers. Micro needling is referred to by many names such as PRP, Vampire facial, Collagen induction therapy and Mesotherapy.

How does Microneedling work?

Microneedling delivered in any of the methods available is essentially, a form of controlled trauma to the skin and capable of generating a years worth of depleted collagen after just one session!

The micro needling head is covered in tiny needles which create micro channels, which we drench in nutrient rich, problem targeting Mesoestetic serums, which thanks to the microchannels, reach the dermal layer of the skin. These channels spontaneously close after 10 minutes so that the protective epidermal barrier remains in tact, allowing the skin to retain the hydration delivered and prevent infection.

Meanwhile deeper below the skins surface, the bodies response to heal and release vital growth factors, which in turn lead to the production of new collagen, elastin and production hyarulonic acid, whilst encouraging cell renewal and cell repair at the dermal layer, improving skin texture, firming and hydrating, reducing scars, tightening the appearance of enlarged pores, reducing pigmentation and problematic skin issues.

What is Meso Therapy?

Meso therapy is a French rejuvenation technique, where micro bolus injections of vitamins, enzymes, antioxidants and hyarulonic acid are delivered into the superficial epidermis to tighten and rejuvenate the skin.

Meso Therapy can instantly improve dull, dehydrated and tired looking skin, help to improve sluggish blood circulation, stimulate fibroblast cells and in turn increase collagen production and feed the skin from below the surface, give a glowing appearance.

Think of Meso Therapy as the fertiliser for your Collagen Production!
