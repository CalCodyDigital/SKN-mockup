Laser Therapy

You shouldn’t have to choose between feeling confident in your skin and feeling comfortable in it.

Unwanted hair, post shave irritation, pigmentation that won’t fade, enlarged pores, old tattoos you’ve outgrown, none of it is “life threatening,” but it chips away at how you feel in your own skin.

At SKN Lab Aesthetics, laser isn’t a quick add-on treatment. It’s a clinical solution for people who are tired of temporary fixes. Every decision is made with intention: is this safe for your skin? Will it work long-term? Is there a better way? If not, we don’t do it.

Laser treatments at SKN Lab are led by Tash, our laser specialist. She works with the Blaze Laser platform, a high performance medical system used for hair removal, skin correction, tattoo removal and carbon facials.

Every setting is customised to your skin type, tone and sensitivity. Nothing is rushed or automated. The focus is always on safety, precision and visible results.

Laser is ideal for clients who want long-term solutions. It can permanently reduce hair growth, reduce pigmentation and redness, remove tattoos and resurface skin where skincare cannot. Tash is known for being calm, honest and thorough. If something will not work for your skin, she will tell you.

What can Laser Treat? For the things you keep covering up, shaving off or learning to live with, laser is where they finally stop controlling you.

UNWANTED HAIR AND INGROWNS

- Shaving rash bikini burn coarse dark hair growing back within days. Laser targets the follicle to slow and eventually stop regrowth — no more irritation or constant shaving.

PIGMENTATION AND SUN DAMAGE

If you’re covering patches with concealer every morning or sunlight makes dark spots show more. Laser breaks down excess pigment gradually so skin starts to look clearer and more even.

REDNESS, BROKEN VESSELS & ROSACEA

- Flushed cheeks visible capillaries rosacea flare ups that make skin feel hot and reactive. IPL reduces blood vessels beneath the skin and calms that constant red look — especially on cheeks nose and chin.

TATTOO OR SPMU REGRET?

- An ex’s name old eyebrow tattoo stuck in 2012 SMP that’s turned blue or patchy. Laser breaks down the pigment  particle by particle so your body clears it naturally — no cutting no scarring.

DULL, UNEVEN SKIN TEXTURE

- Makeup sitting in pores skin that looks grey flat or tired no matter what skincare you use. Carbon laser facials and rejuvenation treatments resurface the skin tighten pores and restore natural clarity and glow.

Specialist Led Laser at SKN Lab Aesthetics

You’re not treated by a machine or left to figure things out alone. All laser treatments at SKN Lab are carried out by Tash, a medical professional and laser specialist who works independently within our clinic.

What makes her different is that she won’t treat you unless it’s safe and she knows it will work. If laser isn’t right for your skin tone, hormones, medication or hair type, she will tell you before you spend anything.

There is no conveyor belt approach here. Every setting is adjusted by hand, every session is tracked and every result is monitored. You’re remembered, not rushed.

If you’re ready to stop shaving, hiding, or feeling like your skin is out of your control, this is where things start to change. You can book a consultation patch test online or send a message if you’re not sure where to begin.
