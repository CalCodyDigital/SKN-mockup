Because the right treatment starts with the right conversation.

At SKN Lab Aesthetics®, every journey begins with a consultation.

Not because we want to sell you a treatment, but because we want to understand what's brought you here.

You may be concerned about ageing, skin quality, facial volume loss or simply feel that your reflection no longer matches how you feel. Whatever your reason, your consultation gives us the opportunity to assess your concerns, discuss your goals and create a bespoke treatment plan tailored entirely to you.

With over 21 years of experience in aesthetics, we'll guide you honestly through your options, explain what is achievable and recommend only the treatments we genuinely believe will benefit you.

No pressure. No confusion. Just expert advice, personalised recommendations and a clear plan forward.
