Skin Boosters

Your skin, rehydrated, rejuvenated and restored from within

At SKN Lab Aesthetics® in Ware, “ skin booster ” is an umbrella term that covers a range of injectable treatments designed to hydrate, smooth and strengthen the skin at every level.

Unlike traditional dermal fillers, skin boosters do not change facial shape or add volume. Instead, they focus on improving skin quality, health and radiance from the inside out.

Every treatment plan is individually tailored by our expert practitioners to suit your skin type, lifestyle and goals.

Hyaluronic Acid SKN Boosters

Hydration. Glow. Elasticity .

Hyaluronic Acid (HA) skin boosters are the foundation of injectable skincare. They deliver deep, long-lasting hydration that improves skin tone, elasticity and texture while restoring a youthful, healthy glow.

At SKN Lab Aesthetics®, we use a curated selection of premium HA-based boosters, including: Profhilo®, the gold-standard treatment for remodelling and luminosity Seventy Hyal 2000, a potent hydrator that restores plumpness and radiance Jalupro®, ideal for refining texture and brightening the under-eye area Dorothy Dewy, a new-generation HA formula for long-term moisture and a smooth, airbrushed complexion

Your practitioner will select the most suitable formulation to deliver visible, natural results based on your unique skin needs.

Polynucleotide SKN Boosters

Repair. Regenerate. Restore.

Polynucleotides are advanced bioactive molecules derived from purified DNA fragments. They help repair tissue, calm inflammation and improve the structure and function of the skin.

These treatments promote collagen synthesis, accelerate cellular turnover and strengthen the skin barrier, making them ideal for tired, dull or inflamed complexions.

At SKN Lab Aesthetics®, we use leading polynucleotide formulations designed to rejuvenate and repair skin at a cellular level.

Profhilo® Structura & Kairax Enhance 45

Hydration. Support. Structural restoration.

At SKN Lab Aesthetics®, we offer a carefully selected range of advanced injectables from the Profhilo® and Kairax families, each designed to target different layers of the skin and facial structure.

Profhilo® sits within the skin booster category, working to deeply hydrate and remodel the skin to improve elasticity, glow and firmness. It’s the perfect choice for enhancing skin quality without altering your natural facial contours.

Profhilo® Structura and Kairax Enhance 45 target the adipose (fat pad) layer to rebuild support where it has diminished over time. They help lift and define areas such as the mid-face and jowls while strengthening the underlying foundation of the skin.

Both treatments stimulate collagen and elastin to restore firmness and resilience. They achieve similar results but are never used together. Your practitioner will recommend the most suitable option for your goals and comfort.

Exosomes

Regeneration. Communication. Cellular rejuvenation.

Exosomes are microscopic messengers naturally produced by our own cells. They carry vital molecular information that tells skin cells how to repair, renew and regenerate.

When applied to the skin, exosomes enhance cell-to-cell communication, boosting collagen and elastin production, accelerating healing, calming inflammation and improving overall vitality.

At SKN Lab Aesthetics®, we often pair Exosome therapy with microneedling or Fusion 8 treatments to enhance delivery and maximise regenerative results. The combination helps refine texture, improve luminosity and support the skin’s natural recovery.

Exosomes can also be performed as a standalone treatment for clients seeking complete cellular-level rejuvenation and long-term skin health.

Biostimulators (PLLA: Sculptra® & Aesplla®)

The collagen architects.

PLLA biostimulators such as Sculptra® and Aesplla® work by activating your body’s natural collagen production to gradually restore structure, firmness and elasticity.

Rather than filling lines directly, they rebuild your skin’s support network from within, creating a lifted, youthful look that develops gradually and lasts longer.

These treatments are ideal for clients seeking subtle, natural rejuvenation and long-term strengthening of the skin’s architecture.
