The Science behind your expression lines

Every smile, frown and squint leaves a small imprint on your skin.

As collagen, elastin and hydration slow down, those expressions begin to stay longer than you’d like.

Anti-wrinkle injections work beneath the skin’s surface to relax specific muscles. The skin above them smooths naturally, and lines begin to fade over time. It’s not about erasing movement, it’s about bringing back balance.

Step Inside the SKN Method

Step 1 - Personal Consultation

Every treatment begins with a complimentary, detailed consultation. We take time to understand your goals, how your face moves, and what’s truly needed to restore natural balance. This stage is about honesty, education, and precision - not upselling or rushing. You’ll leave your consultation with a clear, realistic plan designed just for you.

Step 2 - Prescriber Consultation

All wrinkle-relaxing treatments at SKN Lab Aesthetics® require a face-to-face consultation with our in-house qualified prescriber. This ensures your treatment is medically appropriate, safe, and individually tailored. Your prescriber will review your medical history, assess your muscle strength, and prescribe the exact dose needed for your facial structure and desired result.

Step 3 - Your Treatment

Once prescribed, your treatment is carried out using premium Botulinum Toxin brands — Botox®, Azzalure®, and Bocouture® — carefully selected for your skin, expression, and goals. Precise micro-injections relax overactive muscles beneath the skin, allowing fine lines to smooth and the face to appear calmer and more refreshed. Results begin to show gradually, with full effect visible around two weeks.

Step 4 - Review

Your treatment doesn’t end when you leave the clinic. A complimentary review at two weeks ensures your results have settled perfectly and allows for any fine-tuning if required. This step is part of our commitment to balance, safety, and achieving your ideal finish. Most clients choose maintenance treatments every 3–4 months to keep their results smooth and effortless.
