Private Blood Testing

Our Blood Testing Services

Prices from £50

Here at SKN Lab Aesthetics™ we offer a vast array of blood tests covering Allergy tests to Full hormone profiles for men or women.Our private blood testing at our Clinic in Ware, is quick and discreet. Clients will receive a comprehensive doctor’s report by email with the result of every test and how they compare with standard reference ranges. All test results are reviewed by our in-house GPs who comment on any abnormal result, highlighting anything that may need further investigation or follow-up.

Whatever your reason we have a solution - whilst we have a comprehensive list of blood tests available, we work directly with a laboratory and can on occasion facilitate other blood tests, by providing the bio markers required. Please get in touch to discuss your requirements.

**Please note we do not diagnose any medical conditions and if your levels are out of abnormal range we recommend you present the test to your GP for further investigation.
